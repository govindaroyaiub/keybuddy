Why KeyBuddy

Small app. Big thock.

Every app

Docs, code, chat, email. KeyBuddy lives in your menu bar or system tray and plays along everywhere.

Record your own board

Type a sentence near your mic. KeyBuddy knows which key you pressed, so every sound lands on the right key.

Sounds like a real board

Every key keeps its own take, space and enter sound bigger, and releases click back like real switches.

Stereo by key position

Left-hand keys come from the left, right-hand keys from the right. Put on headphones and listen.

Private by design

It only knows which key moved, never what you typed. No account, no analytics, nothing leaves your computer.

Light as a feather

A small native app with a tiny audio mixer. Sounds start within a few milliseconds of your keypress.
